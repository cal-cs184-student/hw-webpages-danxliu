Low vs High \(k_s\)

I varied the spring constant on the scene/pinned2.json file. Using a very low \(k_s\) causes the cloth to droop down very low, since a low spring constant leads to low stiffness. Using a very high \(k_s\) causes cloth to only bend a little at the top, since a high spring constant leads to high stiffness.

Low vs High Density

I varied the density on the scene/pinned2.json file. Using a very high density causes the cloth to droop down very low, since the cloth is weighed down more. Using a very low density causes the cloth to only bend down a little, since the cloth is not as weighted down.

Low vs High Damping

I varied the damping on the scene/pinned2.json file. Using a very high damping causes the cloth to become very slow when moving, and not sway at all like a solid object. Using a very low damping causes the cloth to sway intensely and move very fast, like it was made out of very loose material.
